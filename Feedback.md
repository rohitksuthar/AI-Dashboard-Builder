## Example

**Type:** Bug / Error

**Description:** Dashboard chart does not update after changing the date range.

***Steps to Reproduce:***
1. Open the dashboard.
2. Change the date range.
3. Click Apply.

**Expected Result:** Chart should update with the selected date range.

**Actual Result:** Chart continues showing the previous data.

**Suggestion:** Refresh the chart automatically after applying the filter.
