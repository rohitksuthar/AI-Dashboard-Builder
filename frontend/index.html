FEATURES

Everything a founder needs to read their own numbers

No analyst required. Drop in a CSV and InsightBoard handles the rest, entirely in your browser.

Automatic Dashboard Generation

Every upload becomes a full dashboard — KPIs, charts and tables — without any manual setup.

Smart KPI Detection

InsightBoard recognizes sales, profit, quantity and order columns and turns them into KPI cards automatically.

Interactive Charts

Trend lines, product breakdowns and regional splits, rendered with Chart.js and fully responsive.

Sales Prediction

Business Insights

Dataset Management
